Results — inherited negative experiment, not a promotion

All performance measurements below are labelled HOLDOUT-DTI. Evaluator: gemsdoe66.metric.dti (the inherited card reports a 1e-9 comparison with a brute-force equation transcription). Instrument: SGMC off-catalogue faults, 8 spatial folds, approximately 7,700 withheld positives per fold (61,664 total), 300 m training-only buffer, α = 0.2, β = 0.8, 300 m triangular kernel. The listed 95% CIs are the recorded percentile intervals over folds. This proxy holdout is not the organizer's hidden set. No ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED score exists for any artifact.

Protocol deviation: the inherited lane used 1 m-derived 100 m descriptors and 2–20 pixel scales on the 100 m grid (200–2,000 m), not the requested native-resolution 2–20 m scarp method. The numeric results below do not validate LIDAR-01.

H66-C (2026-10-12) — native-resolution LiDAR scarp channels

Method: diffused-scarp template inversion on the 2 m-computed LiDAR descriptors (a_hat ∝ S²/C, κt_hat ∝ a_hat²/S²), Steger sub-pixel lines and Frangi multiscale vesselness at σ = 1.0–3.0 cells, an official TIGER 2024 road mask, gradient-boosted field under 20 km block holdout, and a 200 m hard / 600 m ramp catalogue-gap gate. 63 features; leakage canary highest single-feature AUC 0.646 against a 0.90 threshold.

Instrument validity — the decisive measurement

Every holdout instrument was scored against the 16 registry rasters that carry an owner-reported leaderboard value:

Consequence. No local instrument in this repository is a validated surrogate for the organizer's hidden score. That is a structural fact, not a tuning failure: the scored truth is a hidden set of expert-identified faults that is explicitly disjoint from every public inventory, so no split of public data can reproduce it.
