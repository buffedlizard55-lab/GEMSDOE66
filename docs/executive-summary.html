The literal 3-px overlap test trips only against registry rasters carrying >3x the candidate's dot count: after a 3-px dilation those rasters cover most of the grid, so a RANDOM placement scores at least as high as the candidate (0.979 random vs 0.921 candidate against gems61-lane-buried-step-gravinversion-hgm-r3-k400k-b2zeros, lift 0.94, Spearman rho -0.010). The chance-corrected rule - overlap > 0.70 AND > 2x the random baseline AND comparable density - is the informative one and it does not trip (density-comparable max overlap 0.641).

4 · Caveats you should read before spending a slot

  • HOLDOUT-DTI is an internal hide-and-recover measurement on the public catalogue, not an ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED leaderboard score.
  • The measured gain reverses in the low-relief basin floor (18.3% of withheld positives): mean kernel credit 0.0224 vs 0.0660 for random (lift 0.34). The gain is real in the mid- (2.83x) and high-relief (2.31x) strata, so it is not confined to range fronts, but it is not present everywhere.
  • The Steger/Frangi operators run at sigma = 1.0-4.0 x 100 m (100-400 m), not the requested 2-20 m native scales: the 1 m/2 m bare-earth DEM is not reachable from this environment and the cached 100 m aggregate keeps only per-cell extrema of 2 m descriptors.
  • No cultural-feature mask (roads, canals, field edges) exists in the restored inputs, so the veto is the channel-internal cross-slope/downslope step term only.
  • registry/rasters is a local mirror of 60 rasters; the complete organizer corpus is not certified.
