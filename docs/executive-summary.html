3 · What you are betting on, in one paragraph

The organizers state that the scored faults were identified by experts and are not in the public fault databases. Every mapped network available here — the given catalogue and the state-map compilation — is therefore closer to “already in the database” than to the truth, and the metric charges mass there as false positive while it can earn no credit. This file spends all 40,000 unit-mass dots at least 400 m from every mapped fault (mean 2,336 m), weighted toward the mirrored 1 m-derived scarp channels and the owner-reported habitat direction. If the new faults instead cluster on and beside the mapped networks — the usual outcome in a mature province — the design forfeits that mass. That is the downside; the gate table above is the honest accounting of what this session could and could not measure about it.
