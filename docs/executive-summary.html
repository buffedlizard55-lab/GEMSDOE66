Executive summary — why there is nothing to download

Verdict: NEGATIVE — do not submit this file

A complete, format-valid GeoTIFF exists in artifacts/lidar_lane/. It is not published as a download because the last experiment overturned the result that would have justified publishing it.

The finding. The holdout truth (SGMC faults > 300 m from the given catalogue) is not distributed like the terrain: it is 59.3 % range front and 1.3 % basin, while the footprint is 25.2 % range front and 23.7 % basin. Mapped faults are concentrated on range fronts because scarps are visible there, not because faults are. Any scarp field therefore "wins" on a mapped-fault truth simply by matching the truth's relief prior. Re-scoring with the confound removed — budget allocated across relief strata in proportion to footprint area, then averaged with footprint weights — gives:

4 · Is it OK to download and submit?

Yes, and here is the exact basis for that answer — including what is not claimed.
  • Format: verified, on the bytes, by re-reading the written file. Receipt: artifacts/lidar_lane/checks--nan.tif.json.
  • Originality: the duplicate gate against all 60 registry rasters returns UNIQUE (max |ρ| = 0.28 on the dots, 0.08 on the surface, against a 0.90 threshold). It is a new raster, not a copy.
  • NOT demonstrated: on the relief-matched instrument the lane is indistinguishable from the incumbent (−0.0009, CI [−0.0084, +0.0066]) and from uniform random placement (+0.0003, CI [−0.0055, +0.0060]). On range fronts — the stratum the lane was built for — it scores 0.0505 against 0.0487 for random dots at the same budget.
  • NOT claimed: no organiser score exists for this file. Every leaderboard number on this site is an owner report or a public-page snapshot. The holdout's truth is SGMC, not the expert panel's hidden set.

4 · What would change the verdict

The lane is not refuted as physics — a tectonic scarp does not survive basin-floor aggradation, and the channel is genuinely strong where relief is high. It is refuted as a decision: the evidence does not separate it from the incumbent, and it sits far outside the family's operating point (64 % of its dots on range fronts, against 17 % for every prior submission). Three things would change that:

  1. A relief-unbiased truth set. Score the lane against faults that are not surface-visible — well intersections, seismic/reflection picks, or geophysically-inferred structures. If the lane still wins there, the confound argument dies and the promotion is real. This is the single most valuable next experiment.
  2. One live score at a moderate range-front fraction. If a submission with, say, 30 % range-front dots scores above 0.2778, the live board rewards relief concentration and a 64 % field becomes a defensible extrapolation rather than a gamble.
  3. A composite that keeps the incumbent's mid-relief skill. The incumbent beats the lane in the mid-relief stratum, which holds 51 % of the footprint and 39 % of the truth (0.0395 vs 0.0263). A hybrid that takes mid-relief from the incumbent and range fronts from the lane is testable on the existing relief-matched instrument and does not need new data.
