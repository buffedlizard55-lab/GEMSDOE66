What this candidate is, in one paragraph

Two statistics of previously scored rasters in this family were validated against the only ground signal available (owner-reported leaderboard scores, n=18, no hash-to-score receipt): higher mean radiometric K under the emitted dots (+0.977, partial +0.913 controlling for dot count and catalogue adjacency) and fewer dots (−0.827) both go with higher scores, and a two-variable model fit to those statistics reaches leave-one-out RMSE 0.0023 against a 0.0249 baseline. The candidate therefore moves all three designable quantities in the validated direction inside the only region where the family has ever scored well: 32,000 dots (the family's floor was 37,654), mean K 126.3 (the family's band was 97.1–101.0), coverage 11.8% (the incumbent's was 15.1%), zero dots within 200 m of the catalogue, and every dot chosen by the published metric's own first-order condition.

The honest limits

  • No HOLDOUT-DTI. NOT MEASURED — the scored truth is login-walled; the visible catalogue is the wrong exam
  • No organiser score. Output-side statistics are projections from a fitted surface whose support is narrower than this candidate; the projection is deliberately not printed as a number here.
  • The 3-px dot-overlap rule is degenerate in this registry (max 0.999; the incumbent itself scores 1.000; zero cells are more than 3 px from every registry dot). The rank-correlation half of the same protocol passes at 0.289 vs a 0.90 limit.
  • Registry completeness is not certified — 60 accessible rasters were compared.
