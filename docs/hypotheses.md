# Ranked lidar-scarp hypotheses

**Status: proposals only.** No feature, holdout, canary, or raster calculation has run in this checkout. The ranking below is a qualitative prior for experiment order—not an estimated score or a DTI projection. Every improvement must be earned on the frozen spatial holdout and reported as `HOLDOUT-DTI` with evaluator version, withheld-positive count, and 95% CI.

## Scope and novelty boundary

All four candidates stay inside the required native-resolution lidar-scarp lane. The input is the 1 m bare-earth GeoDAWN/USGS 3DEP DEM and the existing cultural-feature mask; the output is aggregated to the competition's 100 m grid. The actual competition test labels are expert-labelled *new faults*, not geothermal vents, and are not available here.

The local repository began with only a README, so each proposal differs from **this checkout's** implementation (there was no implementation). That does **not** establish that it is new across the user's sibling GEMSDOE repositories. Public sibling pages already describe 1 m lidar scarp channels, ridge-thinned emissions, and multi-channel scarp consensus. The requested template/Steger/Frangi combination may overlap those rasters. No cross-repository novelty claim is allowed until the continuous candidate surface and final dots have both been compared to the complete prior-raster registry.

## Data availability check

The official competition description says the data tab includes a CSV of 1 m DEM links. That page resolves to a login screen in this environment. The official USGS National Map 3DEP catalog also lists a public seamless 1 m DEM collection, so the source is **publicly obtainable in principle**. However, the exact tiles, footprint coverage, vertical/horizontal alignment, and files needed for this particular run were not fetched or verified here. The candidates are therefore **not runnable or validated in this checkout**. The official competition tile-link CSV, when lawfully available to the registered competitor, is the preferred manifest; do not silently substitute a different DEM product.

The cached stack, cultural mask, evaluator, writer, validator, catalogue raster, holdout segmentation, and prior-raster registry are also absent. Per the reuse protocol, this repo must not create private replacements for them. The highest-ranked candidate was **not** tested; no submission slot was used.

## Confounders to carry into every experiment

- **Road cuts, canals, and field boundaries:** direct cultural linear mimics; mask with the specified existing cultural-feature mask.
- **Natural channel banks, terrace edges, and paleoshorelines:** linear or curvilinear slope breaks that can resemble scarps.
- **Landslides and talus margins:** coherent scarps with plausible curvature but non-tectonic origin.
- **Range-front concentration:** a model can appear useful by re-detecting known, conspicuous topography while failing on less obvious faults; report the required stratified gate.
- **DEM seams, nodata edges, vegetation/artifact texture, and smoothing bias:** can manufacture line responses; test edge handling and constant-slope controls before interpreting geology.
