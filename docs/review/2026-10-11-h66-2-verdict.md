## What was tested

H66-2 replaces the H66-1 weighted rank-blend with a **conjunction**: the geometric mean of
rank(10-vs-50 m slope break `step_max`), rank(1 − dip-sense `facing`), and rank(crest-toe sharpness
`sqrt(lapneg·lappos)`) on the pinned 2 m LiDAR stack. Physical claim: a real fault scarp fires all
three observables at once, whereas channel banks and terrace risers fire the slope break without the
dip-sense, and rounded old scarps fire it without the sharpness. Named non-fault mimic: fluvial
channel banks and terrace risers; controls: terrain-only steepness/relief ranks, which use no LiDAR
texture at all. Preregistration: `registry/preregistration-h66-2.json` (decision rule frozen before
measurement; axis change vs H66-1 is the field, not the emission policy).
