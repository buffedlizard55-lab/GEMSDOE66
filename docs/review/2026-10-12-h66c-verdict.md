## Why the instrument result cannot be read as a score

Two instruments were built and both were then validated against the 16 registry rasters that
carry an owner-reported leaderboard value:

* instrument **N** (USGS SGMC faults >300 m from the catalogue, inside LiDAR-covered held-out
  blocks): Spearman +0.644 (p = 0.0071) with the live score — but &rho;(dots, live) = −0.844 on
  the same set, and with 16 points the partial correlation is not identified.
* instrument **S** (withheld catalogue segments inside held-out blocks): Spearman **−0.708**
  (p = 0.0021). It is anti-correlated by construction: it measures rediscovery of the public
  catalogue, and the organisers' scored truth is explicitly disjoint from that catalogue.

Conclusion: **no local instrument here is a validated surrogate for the hidden score.** This is
structural, not a tuning failure, and it is the most important finding of the session. The gate
in the brief ("DTI gain on lidar-covered holdout blocks that is not confined to range fronts")
therefore cannot be satisfied in any way that is demonstrably predictive, and the candidate is
reported as failing it.

## Defects found and fixed during the session

1. **Frangi vesselness was degenerate.** Estimating Frangi's `c` from the raw image intensity
   collapsed the response (max 0.0155, p99 = 1.9e-4). Fixed by estimating `c` robustly from the
   pooled Hessian norm across all scales and by taking the larger of the ridge and valley
   responses; the fixed channel has a median of 0.064 at the emitted dots.
2. **Footprint convention mismatch.** `sample_submission.tif`'s finite mask (5,167,373 cells)
   and the training-features footprint (5,165,852 cells) differ on 4,601 cells, and neither is a
   subset of the other. The features convention (used by the shared validator and the reference
   solution) is now the one the writer uses. Logged as `IR-66-FOOT-01`.
3. **The BLM closed-claim raster is not a disturbance mask.** 45% of the footprint lies within
   150 m of a closed claim, so the veto was disabled by default: "closed to mineral entry" is an
   administrative status, not a physical disturbance.
4. The collateral damage from (1) and (2) is that an earlier apparent win (N = 0.1079) did not
   survive: with the corrected footprint and the fixed channel the candidate scores
   0.0979. The earlier figure is retained in
   `evidence/h66c_experiment3.json` and flagged as superseded.

## Limitations

* No raw 1 m or 2 m DEM is reachable from this sandbox, so "native resolution" means the
  2 m-computed LiDAR descriptor stack; Scarplet is not re-run on profiles.
* The diffusion-inversion constants are defined only up to the unpinned normalisation of the
  upstream LoG operator, so `a_hat` and `kt_hat` are used through rank transforms only.
* Only 60 registry rasters were scanned; the organiser's complete registry is not public.
* No independent scientific-lead review was obtained.

## Recommendation

Do not spend a submission slot on this artifact unless a selector independently judges that a
unique, format-clean alternative lane is worth a draw. The headroom identified by this session
is in **placement geometry** (the catalogue-gap dose response), not in the new scarp physics:
the candidate is at parity-to-slightly-worse on detection while carrying a different, verified
information source.

## Uniqueness — one number needs a caveat

The candidate is unique on every density-adjusted diagnostic over all 60 accessible registry
rasters: surface |ρ| ≤ 0.080, final-dot |ρ| ≤ 0.290, highest lift over random placement 2.46×,
and the worst within-3 px fraction among the 48 rasters that exceed 1.5× random is 0.341 (0.70
stop). Full per-raster table: `evidence/uniqueness_h66c_full.json`.

The literal protocol wording — "more than 70% of your dots fall within 3 px of one registry
raster" — does trip, at 0.994, against `gems61-lane-buried-step-gravinversion-hgm-r3-k400k-b2zeros.tif`.
That raster has 311,350 dots (6.0% of the footprint), so random placement already puts 95.8% of
locations within 3 px of one of them; the lift over random is 1.04×. The rule trips on density,
not similarity — the failure mode already registered as IR-66-GATE-01. It is logged as
**IR-66-UNIQ-01** and displayed on the site as a WARN rather than silently converted into a PASS,
because IR-71 established for the inherited lane that a density-adjusted verdict must not
overrule the raw threshold.
