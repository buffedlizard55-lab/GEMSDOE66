## 2. What was run

Lane: native-resolution LiDAR scarp channels, executed **at the 100 m cell scale** on the mirrored
1 m-derived descriptor channels because the raw 1 m GeoDAWN/3DEP tiles are unreachable from this
environment (IR-67-DATA-01). Method as pre-registered in `registry/preregistration-h67.json`:
lane aggregates (scarp-pixel fraction, SNR-weighted amplitude, tensor/orientation coherence,
dominant strike), a scale-persistence term, an owner-reported-anchored habitat direction, a hard
400 m exclusion around every mapped fault (catalogue ∪ SGMC), and a density-equalising systematic
sample with a 3 px minimum separation emitting exactly 40,000 dots.

Three pre-registered experiments were used, all of them instrument-building:

1. **E1 credit regression** — `scripts/h67_e1_credit_regression.py` →
   `evidence/h67_e1_credit_regression.json`. 15 owner-named rasters matched by a token-Jaccard
   matcher; layer dot-means regressed on the owner-reported score with leave-one-out sign stability.
2. **E1b robustness** — `scripts/h67_e1b_robustness.py` → `evidence/h67_e1b_robustness.json`
   (spatial confound, partial correlations controlling `col_mean`, LOO, credit-vs-\|G\| scale).
3. **E2 proxy-vs-live concordance** — `evidence/h67_e2_proxy_concordance.json`: no available truth
   proxy ranks this family the way the owner-reported scores do (catalogue −0.554, SGMC-all
   −0.872, every off-catalogue variant \|rho\| ≤ 0.28).

Consequence, and the single most important result of the session: **the internal holdout cannot
gate a submission for this target.** A gate that requires “a DTI gain not confined to range
fronts” presupposes a truth layer that is not itself range-front-confined; the only truth layer
available is, and the same instrument anti-correlates with the outcome. The gate is recorded as
*unevaluable*, not as passed.

## 6. Remaining work and limitations (for the next session)

1. **Native-resolution execution** (the brief's actual method): requires an authorised environment
   with 1 m GeoDAWN/3DEP tiles, the 10 m DEM channel-steepness layers, and a cultural-feature mask.
2. **A usable instrument**: either a truth layer that is not the exclusion complement, or organizer
   feedback. Until then every internal DTI for this target is a negative control, not a score.
3. **Registry completeness**: 60 rasters compared; the family's 655-raster corpus across 56 sibling
   repositories is not present, so no uniqueness claim can be certified.
4. **Anchor quality**: 15 matched artifacts, 15 of 78 owner names matched, score spread 0.19–0.28,
   two conflicting “best” claims, no receipt.
5. **Amendment hygiene**: the emitted field’s weights follow E1 signs; a future run must either
   pre-register that weighting rule or re-run the lane with the brief’s amplitude-led weighting and
   compare the two emissions at equal mass.
6. **Sensitivity analyses not run**: exclusion radius (2/3/4/5 px), dot budget (36–61 k), hedge
   fraction (0/15/30 %) — each is cheap and would quantify how much of the artifact is design choice.
7. **No submission slot has been spent.** Uploading is the owner’s decision; see the top block of
   `README.md` and `docs/executive-summary.html`.
