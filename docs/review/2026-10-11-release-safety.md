## Scientific assessment and H33 attribution

The owner's [GEMSDOE32 page](https://github.com/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE32/blob/main/docs/index.html) identifies H33-2-B2 as a sparse, catalogue-pruned, live-mirror-based candidate and **explicitly calls it UNSCORED** and states no organizer score exists in that repository. This owner page is not an organizer receipt. The user's H33 score association and two differing statements of the overall leader cannot be authenticated against a receipt linked to the exact TIFF hash here. Consequently, **why that exact file earned the alleged value, or whether a new LiDAR file would beat it, cannot be inferred**. The metric's small false-positive coefficient relative to its false-negative coefficient provides a conditional explanation for why a sparse, well-located dot map *could* do well, not causal proof for H33 or a leaderboard forecast. See [official reference solution](https://github.com/drivendataorg/gems-prize-reference-solution) and the source register `../data/sources.json` (S02, S14, S15).

The competition tests fault traces absent from the public catalogue. Surface scarps can reveal such faults even when 100 m elevation summaries erase their cross-profiles; **scarps are not geothermal vents**. Normal fault permeability and vent placement require separate geological evidence. Competing geomorphic processes include terrace risers, stream banks and landslide scarps. A native template fit (amplitude, morphologic age, azimuth, template SNR) and 2–20 m line persistence should be ablated feature by feature against these mimics, not replaced by the existing coarse proxy and not expected to raise the metric without a measured gain. The four ranked and source-attributed hypotheses are in [`../data/hypotheses.json`](../data/hypotheses.json): LIDAR-01 joint fit (very high cost, ranked first); LIDAR-02 template-only (high); LIDAR-03 signed line-only (high); LIDAR-04 ordered cross-profiles (medium-high). Every projected gain is **unknown**. Official public-domain 3DEP 1 m DEMs are available *in principle* via [USGS's collection record](https://data.usgs.gov/datacatalog/data/USGS:77ae0551-c61e-4979-aedd-d797abdcde0e); no claim is made that exact competition tiles, the cultural-feature mask, or the authorized DEM-link inventory are available in this checkout. Paper references: [Scarplet, JOSS DOI](https://doi.org/10.21105/joss.01066), [Steger DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/34.659930), [Frangi DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BFb0056195). These are method references, **not validation on GEMS**.
