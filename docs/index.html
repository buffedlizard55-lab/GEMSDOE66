What this is

An auditable pipeline that produces one GeoTIFF of fault-probability predictions for the DOE GEMS Prize, plus the evidence for why it looks the way it does. Caveats

  • HOLDOUT-DTI is an internal hide-and-recover measurement on the public catalogue, not an ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED leaderboard score.
  • The measured gain reverses in the low-relief basin floor (18.3% of withheld positives): mean kernel credit 0.0224 vs 0.0660 for random (lift 0.34). The gain is real in the mid- (2.83x) and high-relief (2.31x) strata, so it is not confined to range fronts, but it is not present everywhere.
  • The Steger/Frangi operators run at sigma = 1.0-4.0 x 100 m (100-400 m), not the requested 2-20 m native scales: the 1 m/2 m bare-earth DEM is not reachable from this environment and the cached 100 m aggregate keeps only per-cell extrema of 2 m descriptors.
  • No cultural-feature mask (roads, canals, field edges) exists in the restored inputs, so the veto is the channel-internal cross-slope/downslope step term only.
  • registry/rasters is a local mirror of 60 rasters; the complete organizer corpus is not certified.

The two facts this session measured

1. The official metric is a budget. With T = TP_w, S = Σp, M = Σp·max_g k and |G| truth pixels the organiser formula collapses exactly to

DTI = T / ( 0.2·N + 0.8·|G| )        for unit-mass dots (M = T, S = N)

adding mass raises DTI  ⇔  k > 0.2 · DTI     (a bar of 0.056 at DTI = 0.28)

Two consequences drive the design: the false-positive cost of a dot depends only on how many dots there are, never on where they are; and mass is profitable almost anywhere once the bar is that low. Expected DTI is therefore maximised by maximising the sum of expected kernel credit, with the local dot density following the posterior rather than being drained into a few peaks.

2. The available holdout instruments are anti-correlated with the outcome. Over the 14 registry rasters whose owner-reported names match, proxy DTI against SGMC inventories correlates −0.87 (SGMC-all), −0.55 (catalogue) and −0.17 (LiDAR-restricted off-catalogue) with the owner-reported value. Optimising that gate would push the project backwards, so this session inverted its own release rule and reports the instrument instead of chasing it. See results.

What is in the file

Native-resolution LiDAR scarp channels on the high-prior (LiDAR-covered) tiles: scarp-pixel fraction, SNR-weighted amplitude (amplitude × strike coherence), amplitude-weighted circular orientation coherence and dominant strike from the 1 m-derived descriptor stack, plus scale-persistent Steger/Frangi line evidence on the slope-detrended surface. All four are reported per feature in the results leakage canary. The emission is a density-equalising systematic sample of that posterior at the 40,000-dot budget the owner-reported family peaks at, with a hard 400 m exclusion around every mapped fault inventory.

Known coverage gaps, stated plainly: the raw 1 m tiles and the 10 m DEM are unreachable from this sandbox, so the line evidence runs at 100 m-cell scales; the required cultural-feature mask (roads, canals, field edges) does not exist in this checkout (IR-67-MASK-01) and weaker substitutes were used.
