The one idea that matters

The metric is a budget. With T = TPw, S = Σp, M = Σp·maxgk and |G| truth pixels:

DTI = T / ( 0.2·T + 0.2·(S − M) + 0.8·|G| )

adding a unit of mass raises DTI  ⟺  k > 0.2 · DTI

At DTI = 0.28 that bar is 0.056. Mass that cannot clear it is a loss. That is why the best-scoring rasters in this project family are sparse dot sets (~38 k pixels out of 5.17 M) and not dense probability maps.
