Findings

The GEMSDOE32 page describes H33-2-B2 as unscored and says no organizer score exists for its artifacts; no receipt tied to the exact raster bytes/hash is present. A plausible pruning mechanism is only an inference. See the evidence assessment.

  3. Shared tooling is present and was reused, not forked. This branch carries a DTI implementation, holdout splitters, writer, validator and lane-check utility, and additionally vendors src/gems57 byte-identically from the shared template (23/23 sha256, registry/template_provenance.json) — the end-to-end pooled evaluator, grid and gates the gate needed. The cached lidar stack, cultural inputs and raster registry are restored by scripts/fetch_inputs.sh from hash-pinned mirrors and are deliberately not committed.
  4. Data and coverage remain blockers. The competition data tab is login-gated; the exact 1 m DEM tiles and footprint/alignment have not been acquired or checked. No external mirror was used.
  5. One lane gate measured positive, the other negative; no slot was spent. Three experiments were run against a 2-hour budget (overrun, disclosed).
