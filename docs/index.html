The one idea that matters

The metric is a budget. With T = TPw, S = Σp, M = Σp·maxgk and |G| truth pixels:

DTI = T / ( 0.2·T + 0.2·(S − M) + 0.8·|G| )

adding a unit of mass raises DTI  ⟺  k > 0.2 · DTI

For illustration only, at a hypothetical DTI of 0.28 that bar is 0.056. This is an algebra example, not a measured project value, verified H33 score, or prediction. It shows why surplus low-credit mass can reduce the metric; it does not establish an optimal emission budget for the untested native-resolution lane. See the metric derivation.
