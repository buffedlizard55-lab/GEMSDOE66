The two facts this session measured

1. The official metric is a budget. With T = TP_w, S = Σp, M = Σp·max_g k and |G| truth pixels the organiser formula collapses exactly to DTI = T / (0.2·N + 0.8·|G|) for unit-mass dots. Adding mass raises DTI iff kernel credit k > 0.2 · DTI.

2. Multi-scale structural convergence is the unique lane. This session's submission uses a continuous probability field from fused coherence × lineament-density × curvature at three scales — different from all existing dot-based, scarplet-template, or geophysics-only lanes. The physical signature targeted is subtle fault-zone deformation (distributed convergent lineaments, alternating convex/concave curvature). The named non-fault mimic is alluvial-fan distributary networks and drainage antecedence.
