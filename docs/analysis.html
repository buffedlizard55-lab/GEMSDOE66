2. Mechanism (metric algebra, not a score)

From the identity DTI = T / (0.2 (T + S − M) + 0.8 |G|) (see the derivation), an extra dot raises DTI only when its credit k to an uncovered truth cell satisfies k > 0.2 · DTI. At DTI 0.2778 that bar is k = 0.0556, a distance of about 283 m. Dots farther than that from any truth cell lower the score.

The official problem page says the scored test faults are new faults not in the USGS database. So a dot placed on a catalogue fault earns no credit unless a new fault lies within about 283 m of it. That makes the owner's catalogue-flank removal rule a plausible mechanism. It has not been tested, and nothing in the owner's registry shows that the new faults lie more than 283 m from catalogue faults.

  3. Recompute HOLDOUT-DTI for the candidate rule and a no-removal control on the same spatially blocked holdout, with a 95% CI.
  4. If the rule does not beat the control on the holdout, record a negative result.
