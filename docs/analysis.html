Caveats carried by the evidence file itself

  • owner-reported scores are not organiser receipts and no hash-to-score crosswalk exists
  • the rasters share one lineage, so a layer can track a hidden shared cause
  • n = 18; use as a lane prior, never as a score

Design consequence

The released candidate (see overview) is the pre-registered session-66 design in registry66/preregistration-k66.json: consensus support, K0.5 prior, greedy metric-marginal acceptance, 32,000 dots. Its measured statistics move every designable quantity the direction the family's score record rewards (K mean 126.3 vs the family's scored band 97.1–101.0; dot count at the family floor; zero dots near the catalogue). The two-statistic OLS is out of support for it, so no extrapolated score is quoted.

Historical record: an earlier session reviewed the same question and concluded "no defensible score explanation yet" because no receipt-to-hash crosswalk existed.
