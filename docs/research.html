Research protocol and limitations

1 · Metric transcription

The official problem description defines distance-weighted Tversky scoring. With T = TPw, total prediction mass S, weighted mass M, and truth-pixel count |G|:

DTI = T / (0.2T + 0.2(S − M) + 0.8|G|)

The project tests the transcription in tests/test_metric.py. That code-level test is not a competition score and does not authenticate any label raster.

2 · Target method versus inherited proxy

The requested method is native-resolution scarp evidence: slope-aware templates; Steger sub-pixel and Frangi ridge/valley measures at 2–20 m; 100 m aggregation of scarp fraction, SNR-weighted amplitude, orientation coherence and dominant strike; the existing cultural mask; and spatially blocked validation. The exact 1 m tiles and compatibility checks are not available in this checkout, so this target method has not been run.

The inherited PR #13 lidar_lane used 1 m-derived descriptors and line scales at 2–20 pixels on a 100 m grid, i.e. 200–2,000 m. It is scale-deviating, negative after relief-matching, and remains audit-only. Its internal `HOLDOUT-DTI` values and CIs are labelled on the results page; they do not validate the requested method and are not `ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED`.

3 · Holdout, leakage, and release criteria

  • Withhold whole fault segments with a buffer. Derive catalogue features only from visible faults and mask visible faults pixel-exactly.
  • Score pooled DTI on LiDAR-covered held-out blocks with α = 0.2, β = 0.8 and a 300 m triangular kernel; report evaluator version, withheld-positive count and 95% CI.
  • Run feature-alone leakage canaries; AUC above 0.90 is a stop pending proof otherwise.
  • Compare both continuous pre-placement surfaces and final emitted dots against every indexed registry raster. Apply absolute Spearman thresholds and the raw >70% / 3 px emitted-dot stop; random-density lift is diagnostic only. Require an explicit organizer-complete certificate and matching expected count; incomplete access is not a pass.
  • Verify pixel/hash agreement and the exact official CRS, shape, transform, range, and NaN-only outside-footprint contract. A local format receipt is not organizer acceptance.

4 · Evidence status

Official competition inputs and exact DEM-tile coverage remain unverified in this environment. Owner-maintained GitHub mirrors are hash-pinned for local reproducibility but are not organizer-authenticated. The site does not poll leaderboard pages or infer artifact performance from owner reports. No organizer receipt in this checkout confirms a score for any raster.

The inherited PR #13 run was stopped: its relief-matched result does not beat the incumbent, its raw final-dot overlap exceeds the threshold, its complete-registry status is uncertified, and its pre-placement surface comparison is absent. No experiment or submission slot was used in the PR #12 reconciliation.

5 · Trusted method references

  • [official] DrivenData DOE GEMS Prize competition and problem description; the data tab remains login-walled in the recorded audit.
  • [official] USGS 3DEP and USGS GeoDAWN; product context does not prove exact tile coverage.
  • [literature] Sare & Hilley, Scarplet (JOSS), Steger (IEEE TPAMI), and Frangi et al. (MICCAI). These establish methods, not competition performance.
