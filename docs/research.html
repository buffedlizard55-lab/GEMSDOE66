Research protocol, what was actually run, and its limits

1 · Metric transcription

The official problem description defines distance-weighted Tversky scoring. With T = TPw, prediction mass S, weighted mass M and truth count |G|: DTI = T / (0.2T + 0.2(S − M) + 0.8|G|), kernel k(d) = max(1 − d/300 m, 0). A dot of unit mass raises the score while its expected kernel credit exceeds 0.2 × DTI — at DTI 0.28 that is a credit bar of about 0.056, i.e. roughly 283 m. The project tests the transcription in tests/test_metric.py; that is a code test, not a competition score.

2 · What this session ran

Lane: native-resolution lidar scarp channels (executed at the 100 m cell scale - IR-67-DATA-01). Hypothesis, mechanism and mimic are recorded verbatim in registry/preregistration-h67.json and restated in the run card. Three pre-registered experiments were used, all of them instrument-building rather than submission-tuning:

  1. E1 credit regression — layer dot-means versus the 14 matched owner-reported scores (evidence/h67_e1_credit_regression.json).
  2. E1b robustness — spatial confound, partial correlations controlling col_mean, leave-one-out, and the credit-vs-|G| implied scale (evidence/h67_e1b_robustness.json).
  3. E2 proxy-vs-live concordance — does any available truth proxy rank this artifact family the way the owner-reported scores do? Answer: no (evidence/h67_e2_proxy_concordance.json).

3 · Why no internal number can decide this — measured, not asserted

The internal instrument is the SGMC off-catalogue inventory, scored with the shared evaluator (gems57-pooled-hide-v2, 62,122 withheld positives, 300 m training-only buffer, alpha = 0.2, beta = 0.8). Its proxy DTI versus the owner-reported outcome:

The live anchor is an UNVERIFIED relative-ranking instrument, so even a positive ρ would not certify a submission; but a negative one does establish that optimizing this proxy cannot be assumed to raise the owner-reported score. On top of that, geography dominates the live readings: col_mean ρ -0.890, row_std ρ -0.832. Consequently the H67-1 run uses the instrument only as a negative control, and its pooled value is degenerate by construction (IR-67-INSTR-02).

4 · Holdout, leakage and release criteria (unchanged rules)

  • Withhold whole fault segments with a buffer; derive catalogue features from visible faults only; mask visible faults pixel-exactly.
  • Report pooled DTI with evaluator version, withheld-positive count and a 95 % CI, or report why the instrument cannot measure the target (this session: IR-67-INSTR-01).
  • Leakage canary per feature; AUC above 0.90 is a stop. This session's worst: 0.713 (scale persistence).
  • Compare pre-placement surfaces and final dots against every indexed registry raster on both the rank and the raw 3 px rule — and disclose when the lift exception, not the raw rule, is what produces the pass (IR-67-GATE-02).
