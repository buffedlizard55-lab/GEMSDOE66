Signature
Persistent linear warm or cool residuals above a local high-pass threshold, trend within +/-20 deg of a catalogue-derived strike mode (proposed parameters; not tuned)

Rank 2 · H-B · Shoreline and terrace rejection: remove contour-parallel benches from any emission set

Lane
new (not in owner registry)
Data layers
100 m detrended elevation (band 12) and its slope (band 19); any candidate emission raster
Signature
A candidate whose local trend is within 15 deg of the contour direction AND sits on a locally flat bench (low slope, elevation plateau) is removed
Why off-catalogue
Not a fault detector. It protects precision: emitted mass that sits on a mimic earns only the 0.2 penalty, so removing mimics raises DTI wherever they are being emitted.
Named mimic
Pluvial-lake shorelines and fluvial terrace treads (these are the mimic being removed; their local extent is not verified here)
Versus owner registry

Low: uses existing feature bands
