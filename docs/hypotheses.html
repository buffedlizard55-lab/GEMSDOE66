Why H2 failed, and why that is the most useful result of the session. A model trained on off-catalogue SGMC faults scores 0.014 on held-out SGMC faults — worse than random. It has learned the regional geology of the training clusters, not the fault signal: when a whole spatial cluster is withheld, nothing transfers. Any proposal to "train on the faults the catalogue is missing" has to survive that test, and this one did not.
