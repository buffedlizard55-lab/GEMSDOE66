Ranked hypotheses — geological candidates and their status

Read from registry/hypotheses.json. Ranks are a qualitative prior: expected DTI gain per unit implementation cost, unmeasured. No internal instrument can certify a gain for this target (IR-67-INSTR-01, IR-67-CONFOUND-01), so validation is falsification-only — a blocked holdout may reject an idea, never bless one; only an organizer receipt can do that.

Ranking basis (unchanged, verbatim from the register): Pre-implementation qualitative ranking by physical specificity, likely separation from named mimics, and cost. Sign and magnitude of DTI change are unmeasured; no score projection is made. Novelty against the full family registry is not established. Ranks 1-4 are the original same-lane block; ranks 5-9 are the new geological block (G-01..G-05), ranked against each other by expected improvement per unit cost. The two blocks are not compared numerically.
Items LIDAR-01..LIDAR-04 are the original pre-implementation ranking and their text is unchanged; an implementation_status field was added 2026-10-12. Items G-01..G-05 are new geological hypotheses added 2026-10-12, ranked by qualitative expected improvement per unit implementation cost. No expected gain is a DTI projection, and no internal instrument can certify a gain (IR-67-INSTR-01, IR-67-CONFOUND-01).

rank 1 · LIDAR-01

Slope-aware scarp templates plus scale-persistent Steger/Frangi line evidence

layersAuthorized 1 m bare-earth DEM tiles and the shared cached stack; Scarplet-style template outputs (best-fit amplitude, morphologic age, orientation, and template SNR); signed Steger sub-pixel ridge/valley responses and Frangi Hessian-line measures over 2–20 m scales; the existing cultural-feature mask only after its provenance, alignment, and availability are verified. Aggregate physical channel summaries to the competition's 100 m cells.
physical signature / transformA coherent scarp cross-profile fit that allows finite initial and far-field slopes, co-located with ridge/valley responses persisting across neighboring physical scales and aligned along a trace. Template amplitude, morphologic age, orientation, and SNR must be computed by the cited template method rather than approximated by ratios or robust z-scores.
named non-fault mimicFluvial terrace riser. Also test natural channel banks, road/canal embankments, landslide headscarps, shoreline risers, and DEM seams.
difference from the repoThe present H66 code decodes owner-described 100 m stack proxies; it does not implement the actual Scarplet template fit or footprint-wide native Steger/Frangi scale sweep. Sibling owner reports describe LiDAR scarp channels and thinning, so the joined slope-aware template plus signed cross-scale line persistence is a candidate distinction, not a verified novelty claim. The full raster registry is unavailable.
expected gain (qualitative)Highest qualitative prior in this ranking because it combines two complementary physical measurements; sign and size are unknown. Not a DTI projection.
implementation costVery high: tiled DEM/projection and edge handling, multi-orientation and multiscale operators, mask validation, aggregation, per-feature leakage tests, segment-blocked holdout/CI, full-registry scans, and shared-tool corrections. Runtime and storage are unknown until exact tiles are inventoried.
validation ruleNOT RUN. This is the lead to review before implementation. Do not emit a candidate until authorized data, corrected shared holdout/writer, complete registry, independent scientific sign-offs, and budget are available.
data statusBlocked. Official competition rasters, tile list, shared cache, and existing cultural mask are absent. USGS 3DEP 1 m project DEMs are public domain in principle, but exact study-area coverage and compatibility are unverified (S04, S05, S07).
statusIMPLEMENTED as H67-1 (scarp-lane novelty field + density-equalizing systematic sampler). Outcome: 3 of 4 pre-registered predictions met, P3 measured FALSE (block-density CV 0.32 vs the 0.45-0.58 band), science gate UNEVALUABLE (IR-67-INSTR-01), format and lane checks pass with the IR-67-GATE-02 exception. Not promoted.

rank 5 · G-01

Scarp-sharpness (relative-age) ordering gate on the scarp lane

layersL_ex_max, L_step_max, L_lappos_max, L_lapneg_max, L_cross_max, L_relief, L_coh100, L_strike, L_valid (all present in data/processed/stack.f32)
physical signature / transformDimensionless sharpness A = L_ex_max/(L_step_max+eps) and curvature asymmetry (lappos-lapneg)/(lappos+lapneg) per 100 m cell; the AGU 2015 wavelet-template result is that young, gently-dipping scarps show high step response and low diffusion amplitude and are missed by vertical-scarp templates. Transform: per-cell rank, 3x3 smoothing, then gate (multiply) the lane field by A.
why it catches what the catalogue missesThe competition catalogue is dominated by long, well-expressed, already-mapped traces. A sharpness gate selects the young and subtle scarps an expert adds to a state map, and demotes the degraded lineaments that are already in the database - the opposite of a habitat-style density model.
named non-fault mimicFluvial terrace risers and channel cut banks (sharp, single-sided, short); landslide head scarps (sharp but isolated, no along-strike continuity).
difference from the repoNo field in the repository uses an amplitude/sharpness ratio or a relative-age axis at all; previous lanes used L_ex_max and L_step_max directly as amplitude z-scores.
external data sourceNone needed (GeoDAWN 1 m-derived channels already mirrored).
obtainable here?YES (already local)
expected gain (qualitative)highest of the five - it re-weights the existing lane by a physical age discriminator instead of adding new data
implementation costlow
validation ruleFalsification only: blocked whole-component holdout must not reject it; certification needs an organizer receipt (IR-67-INSTR-01).
data statuslayers present; no new download required

rank 6 · G-02

Along-strike persistence of the scarp lane across three scales

layersL_ex_max, L_step_max, L_coh100, L_strike, L_valid
physical signature / transformBox-mean scarp evidence at 3, 5 and 9 cells (300 m, 500 m, 900 m) combined by a persistence product (geometric mean of the three normalised ranks). A tectonic trace persists at every scale; a cultural edge or a single-cell artefact does not.
why it catches what the catalogue missesExpert-added faults are typically shorter and less continuous than map-scale faults, so a persistence test removes the false positives that dominate a naive scarp-density map, without needing any catalogue habitat prior.
named non-fault mimicIrrigation field boundaries (long, straight, mixed orientation) and rail/road grades.
difference from the repoThe repository's coherence channel L_coh100 is single-scale and the lane parts were never combined into a persistence product; the hedge component uses scale persistence of the structure tensor only.
external data sourceNone needed.
obtainable here?YES (already local)
expected gain (qualitative)moderate - sharpens precision at roughly constant recall
implementation costlow

rank 7 · G-03

Cross-trace polarity pairing against the local dip azimuth

layersL_downface_max, L_upface_max, L_relief, L_strike, det_elev_slope (100 m stack), L_valid
physical signature / transformRequire opposed face directions (up-face and down-face) in adjacent cells whose pairing axis is perpendicular to the dip azimuth from det_elev_slope, i.e. a step whose sense is consistent along strike. Transform: signed dot product of face direction with the gradient azimuth, gated by sign agreement over 3 cells.
why it catches what the catalogue missesA tectonic step has a consistent down-thrown side along its length; that is exactly what a catalogue gap hides (short, low-relief scarps). Single-sided geomorphic edges cannot satisfy the pairing.
named non-fault mimicPluvial shoreline terraces (paired but concentric, not aligned to a through-going dip azimuth); eolian dune margins.
difference from the repoNo prior field pairs the up-face and down-face channels; they are used separately in the lane parts.
external data sourceNone needed.
obtainable here?YES (already local)
expected gain (qualitative)moderate - increases precision on the polarity axis, at some recall cost
implementation costlow-moderate
validation ruleFalsification only, as above.
