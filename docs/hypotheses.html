LIDAR-01		Slope-aware scarp templates plus scale-persistent Steger/Frangi line evidence

Authorized 1 m bare-earth DEM tiles and the shared cached stack; Scarplet-style template outputs (best-fit amplitude, morphologic age, orientation, and template SNR); signed Steger sub-pixel ridge/valley responses and Frangi Hessian-line measures over 2–20 m scales; the existing cultural-feature mask only after its provenance, alignment, and availability are verified. Aggregate physical channel summaries to the competition's 100 m cells.

A coherent scarp cross-profile fit that allows finite initial and far-field slopes, co-located with ridge/valley responses persisting across neighboring physical scales and aligned along a trace. Template amplitude, morphologic age, orientation, and SNR must be computed by the cited template method rather than approximated by ratios or robust z-scores.Fluvial terrace riser. Also test natural channel banks, road/canal embankments, landslide headscarps, shoreline risers, and DEM seams.The present H66 code decodes owner-described 100 m stack proxies; it does not implement the actual Scarplet template fit or footprint-wide native Steger/Frangi scale sweep. Sibling owner reports describe LiDAR scarp channels and thinning, so the joined slope-aware template plus signed cross-scale line persistence is a candidate distinction, not a verified novelty claim. The full raster registry is unavailable.Highest qualitative prior in this ranking because it combines two complementary physical measurements; sign and size are unknown. Not a DTI projection.Very high: tiled DEM/projection and edge handling, multi-orientation and multiscale operators, mask validation, aggregation, per-feature leakage tests, segment-blocked holdout/CI, full-registry scans, and shared-tool corrections. Runtime and storage are unknown until exact tiles are inventoried.NOT RUN. This is the lead to review before implementation. Do not emit a candidate until authorized data, corrected shared holdout/writer, complete registry, independent scientific sign-offs, and budget are available.
