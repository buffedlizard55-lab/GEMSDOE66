Hypothesis
Scarplet-template and Hessian-line evidence decoded from the pinned 2 m LiDAR stack (amplitude in m/m, implied height in m, scarp fill fraction, structure-tensor coherence, relative age) concentrates unmapped fault trace better than mass-matched random inside the LiDAR-covered footprint, and masking roads/canals/field edges raises that concentration further because the prior LiDAR arms' poor live scores are attributed to cultural contamination.
Mechanism
A fault the compilation omits still leaves a ~0.3-1.5 m step in the bare-earth surface. The 2 m stack aggregates the max and the mean of the slope-excess over each 100 m cell; their ratio is the fraction of the cell actually scarped, and dividing the amplitude by the 20 km-block robust background turns it into a per-cell SNR. Combining the SNR-weighted amplitude with the coherence of the scarp strike and the crest/toe sharpness selects cells where a real, fresh, near-straight step exists off the mapped network.
