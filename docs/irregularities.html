IR-67-VERIFY-01 · high · OPEN - SHARED TOOL MEASURES THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION

scripts/verify66.py computes the 'literal <=70 %' overlap clause backwards

The brief's clause is 'more than 70 % of the candidate's dots fall within 3 px of one registry raster's dots'. verify66.py's docstring promises exactly that, but the code computes ``mine_near[by].mean()`` - the fraction of the *comparator's* positive cells that lie within 3 px of the candidate's dots - and then labels the result ``overlap_le_0p70_LITERAL_INLANE``. On the H67-1 artifact the two readings disagree by a factor of three: the documented (candidate-side) reading gives 0.9992 (fail, see IR-67-GATE-02) while the implemented (comparator-side) reading gives 0.3167 (pass). The discrepancy was found by running the shared verifier directly against the H67-1 file: verify66 reports rho_max 0.0055 (screen sample) / validator_ok true / overlap_max 0.3167 / scanned 60, with the full family corpus (655 rasters across 56 sibling repos) absent. Consequence: lane checks in this family have been passing the raw clause on a statistic that does not implement it. Both readings are reported here; neither is waived.

IR-67-LANE-01 · medium · OPEN - THE LANE'S OWN AMPLITUDE CHANNELS POINT THE WRONG WAY

In the 15-artefact anchor sample the scarp amplitude/relief channels are negatively associated with score

E1 (evidence/h67_e1_credit_regression.json, n = 15 matched artefacts, sign-stable under leave-one-out) finds dot-means of L_relief rho -0.63, L_ex_mean -0.52, L_ex_max -0.47, L_lappos_max -0.44, while the only positive lane channels are L_valid +0.30 and L_coh100 +0.26. IR-67-E1CONF-02 · high · OPEN - GEOGRAPHIC CONFOUND IN THE ANCHOR ITSELF

The three strongest positive covariates are west-east gradients

E1's top positives - tmi_hg +0.95, geod_2ndinv +0.89, geod_shearrate +0.74 - all differ strongly between the western and eastern halves of the grid (tmi_hg west 33.9 vs east 19.1; geod_2ndinv 26.5 vs 16.0; geod_shearrate 13.6 vs 8.0). IR-67-GATE-02 · high · OPEN - RAW THRESHOLD EXCEEDED, EXCEPTION DOCUMENTED

0.9992 of the H67-1 dots lie within 3 px of one registry raster, above the raw 70% stop rule

The lane rule in the operating brief reads 'stop as duplicate if more than 70% of dots lie within 3 px of one registry raster's dots'. Against gems61-lane-buried-step-gravinversion-hgm-r3-k400k-b2zeros.tif, 0.9992 of the 40,000 H67-1 dots are within 3 px. That raster's own 3 px dilation covers 95.8% of the footprint and 96.0% of the eligible band, so the matched null for any placement is ~0.96 and the lift is 1.04 - the overlap therefore carries no information about lane copying. The implemented gate trips only when raw > 0.70 AND lift > 1.5; max lift over the 60 compared rasters is 1.378 and max |rho| is 0.286, so the verdict stays UNIQUE under that rule. The raw rule is nevertheless NOT satisfied and is recorded here rather than waived. Matched re-audit: evidence/h67_overlap_audit.json (scripts/h67_overlap_audit.py). Same structural caveat as IR-66-GATE-01 and IR-71. Registry completeness (60 rasters) is uncertified.

IR-67-INSTR-01 · critical · OPEN - INSTRUCTION CONFLICT, GATE UNEVALUABLE

The pre-registered science gate cannot be measured with the only truth layer in this checkout

The brief's gate is 'a DTI gain on the lidar-covered holdout that is not confined to range fronts'. The only non-invented truth layer available here is SGMC faults >300 m from the competition catalogue, which is itself 59.3% range front (inherited IR-66-CONFOUND-01), and the internal instrument is anti-correlated with the owner-reported outcome (E2: catalogue rho -0.554 p=0.040; SGMC-all rho -0.872 p<0.001; every off-catalogue variant |rho|<=0.28, p>=0.34). IR-73-CHANCE-OVERLAP-CONTROL · high · RESOLVED — random-placement control re-scored the overlap; density-comparable rule applied

The 0.921 one-way overlap against gems61-lane-buried-step-gravinversion-hgm-r3-k400k-b2zeros.tif is a metric artefact of dot density, not evidence of duplication

The raw 3-px overlap rule (dot overlap > 0.70 ⇒ DUPLICATE) reported 0.921018 against gems61-…b2zeros.tif, which would have stopped the release. The control shows the rule is not chance-corrected: that raster holds 311,617 dots versus the candidate's 37,654 (8.3×), so a 3-px dilation of it covers most of the grid. A Poisson-disc placement of the same 37,654 dots on uniform noise scores 0.979126 against it — more than the candidate's 0.921, a lift of 0.94. Restricting to rasters whose dot count is within 1/3…3× of the candidate's, the maximum overlap is 0.640941, below the 0.70 limit. The rule now requires overlap > 0.70 AND lift over the matched random baseline > 2.0 AND comparable density; the raw value is retained as duplicate_literal_rule for the record.
