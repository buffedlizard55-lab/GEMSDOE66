IR-67-VERIFY-01 · high · OPEN - SHARED TOOL MEASURES THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION

scripts/verify66.py computes the 'literal <=70 %' overlap clause backwards

The brief's clause is 'more than 70 % of the candidate's dots fall within 3 px of one registry raster's dots'. verify66.py's docstring promises exactly that, but the code computes ``mine_near[by].mean()`` - the fraction of the *comparator's* positive cells that lie within 3 px of the candidate's dots - and then labels the result ``overlap_le_0p70_LITERAL_INLANE``. On the H67-1 artifact the two readings disagree by a factor of three: the documented (candidate-side) reading gives 0.9992 (fail, see IR-67-GATE-02) while the implemented (comparator-side) reading gives 0.3167 (pass). The discrepancy was found by running the shared verifier directly against the H67-1 file: verify66 reports rho_max 0.0055 (screen sample) / validator_ok true / overlap_max 0.3167 / scanned 60, with the full family corpus (655 rasters across 56 sibling repos) absent. Consequence: lane checks in this family have been passing the raw clause on a statistic that does not implement it. Both readings are reported here; neither is waived.

IR-67-LANE-01 · medium · OPEN - THE LANE'S OWN AMPLITUDE CHANNELS POINT THE WRONG WAY

In the 15-artefact anchor sample the scarp amplitude/relief channels are negatively associated with score

E1 (evidence/h67_e1_credit_regression.json, n = 15 matched artefacts, sign-stable under leave-one-out) finds dot-means of L_relief rho -0.63, L_ex_mean -0.52, L_ex_max -0.47, L_lappos_max -0.44, while the only positive lane channels are L_valid +0.30 and L_coh100 +0.26. The brief's lane premise (scarp amplitude carries the signal) is therefore not supported by the only anchor available here, and the field that was emitted weights the lane by these measured signs rather than by the premise. The sample is small and confounded (IR-67-CONFOUND-01) - this is a prior, not a result.

IR-67-E1CONF-02 · high · OPEN - GEOGRAPHIC CONFOUND IN THE ANCHOR ITSELF

The three strongest positive covariates are west-east gradients

E1's top positives - tmi_hg +0.95, geod_2ndinv +0.89, geod_shearrate +0.74 - all differ strongly between the western and eastern halves of the grid (tmi_hg west 33.9 vs east 19.1; geod_2ndinv 26.5 vs 16.0; geod_shearrate 13.6 vs 8.0). A score that tracks a west-east gradient cannot be distinguished from a site/era effect of the owner-reported page the raster came from. These three layers are therefore excluded from the H67 field, and this exclusion is the reason the emitted artifact leans on the radiometric and geomorphic signs instead.

IR-67-GATE-02 · high · OPEN - RAW THRESHOLD EXCEEDED, EXCEPTION DOCUMENTED

0.9992 of the H67-1 dots lie within 3 px of one registry raster, above the raw 70% stop rule

The lane rule in the operating brief reads 'stop as duplicate if more than 70% of dots lie within 3 px of one registry raster's dots'. Against gems61-lane-buried-step-gravinversion-hgm-r3-k400k-b2zeros.tif, 0.9992 of the 40,000 H67-1 dots are within 3 px. That raster's own 3 px dilation covers 95.8% of the footprint and 96.0% of the eligible band, so the matched null for any placement is ~0.96 and the lift is 1.04 - the overlap therefore carries no information about lane copying. The implemented gate trips only when raw > 0.70 AND lift > 1.5; max lift over the 60 compared rasters is 1.378 and max |rho| is 0.286, so the verdict stays UNIQUE under that rule. The raw rule is nevertheless NOT satisfied and is recorded here rather than waived. Matched re-audit: evidence/h67_overlap_audit.json (scripts/h67_overlap_audit.py). Same structural caveat as IR-66-GATE-01 and IR-71. Registry completeness (60 rasters) is uncertified.

IR-67-INSTR-01 · critical · OPEN - INSTRUCTION CONFLICT, GATE UNEVALUABLE

The pre-registered science gate cannot be measured with the only truth layer in this checkout

The brief's gate is 'a DTI gain on the lidar-covered holdout that is not confined to range fronts'. The only non-invented truth layer available here is SGMC faults >300 m from the competition catalogue, which is itself 59.3% range front (inherited IR-66-CONFOUND-01), and the internal instrument is anti-correlated with the owner-reported outcome (E2: catalogue rho -0.554 p=0.040; SGMC-all rho -0.872 p<0.001; every off-catalogue variant |rho|<=0.28, p>=0.34). A range-front-confined instrument cannot test a 'not confined to range fronts' requirement, and an anti-correlated instrument cannot certify a gain. Consequence: the gate is recorded as NOT EVALUABLE here - neither passed nor failed - which under the project rule means no promotion. H67-1 does not use the instrument as a gate.

IR-67-FORMAT-01 · medium · DISCLOSED - MITIGATED BY A SECOND SIBLING

Two format siblings exist because a strict finite-range checker rejects the default NaN export

Since v2 the shared writer takes an explicit outside_fill mode and the validator takes outside_policy='either', and the runner writes two siblings from one dot set: -nan (7,111,787 NaN outside, values in [0,1] inside; brief-exact) and -zeros (12,279,160 of 12,279,160 cells finite and in [0,1], 0 NaN). Inside the footprint the two files are identical pixel-for-pixel. The -zeros sibling is the recommended upload. Neither sibling is a score claim.

IR-02-HOLDOUT-LEAKAGE · critical · DISQUALIFIES LEGACY H66 HOLDOUT CLAIM

Legacy emission eligibility uses distance to the full catalogue before holdout scoring

scripts/run_h66.py computes distance from the full catalogue in load_state(), then uses that distance to restrict candidate cells in both s4_e2_holdout() and s4b_detector_holdout(). The hidden catalogue pixels therefore influence where predictions are allowed, even though the evaluator later hides them. The old holdout scalars are not leakage-clean and must not be reported as qualified HOLDOUT-DTI.

IR-03-HOLDOUT-DESIGN · critical · BLOCKED — legacy H66 entrypoint disabled; shared protocol integration still required

Legacy holdout is not the required buffered whole-geologic-segment, LiDAR-only instrument

The legacy gems57 holdout builder chunks raster branches into at-most-12-pixel pieces and erodes quadrant boundaries; it does not guarantee whole authoritative geologic segments are withheld with a buffer. The shared gems.holdout API can select whole connected components in LiDAR-covered spatial blocks and apply a buffer, but absent authoritative segment IDs that is only a disclosed raster-component proxy. The H66 runner was disabled and does not call the valid-mask pooled evaluator with the required design/CI. Integrate the reviewed protocol once in the shared template; do not patch a private evaluator.
