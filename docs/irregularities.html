IR-66-CONFOUND-01
verdict-changing		 The primary instrument was confounded, and the confound was the whole result. The novelty holdout truth (SGMC faults > 300 m from the catalogue) is 59.3 % range front and 1.3 % basin, against a footprint that is 25.2 % range front and 23.7 % basin. Mapped faults concentrate where scarps are visible, so any range-front-heavy field beats a uniform one on a mapped-fault truth by construction. The lane puts 64.3 % of its dots on range fronts (the incumbent: 17.2 %), i.e. it matches the truth's own relief prior. De-confounded — budget allocated across relief strata in proportion to footprint area, per-stratum DTI averaged with footprint weights — the lane scores 0.02638 against the incumbent's 0.02727 and random's 0.02612: lane−incumbent −0.00089 (CI [−0.0084, +0.0066], 3/8 folds), lane−random +0.00026 (CI [−0.0055, +0.0060], 4/8 folds). The confound is worth +0.0227 — the entire +0.0218 headline. On range fronts specifically the lane's 0.0505 barely exceeds random placement's 0.0487 at the same budget. The run card's verdict was therefore changed from promote to negative and the GeoTIFF moved out of docs/downloads/ into artifacts/lidar_lane/, unpublished. The pooled numbers reproduce exactly (0.07075 / 0.04896), so only the de-confounding is new, not the pipeline. OPEN — verdict flipped
