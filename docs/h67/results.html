1. Metric structure, recovered from the official formula

Because every artifact in the pinned scored mirror is strictly binary (p ∈ {0,1}, asserted before use), the official DTI collapses to

DTI = T / (0.2 T + 0.2 (N − M) + 0.8 |G|)

with N emitted cells, T = TPw, M the emitted mass that lands within 300 m of truth, and |G| the hidden positive count. Writing ρ = T/|G| (kernel-weighted recall) and x = (N−M)/|G| (wasted mass),

DTI = ρ / (0.2 ρ + 0.2 x + 0.8)

Inverting the two scored thinning levels of one belief surface (h25-1-dotted-h19-5 at 60,069 dots → 0.2477 and 44,090 dots → 0.2600) identifies ρ = 0.2565 and x = 0.676 for that family, and these two ratios are invariant to the single nuisance (the wasted-mass fraction). |G| itself is only bounded (≤ 65,241); that is stated rather than guessed. See evidence/h67_truth_size.json.

Consequence. There are exactly two levers: raise ρ, or cut x. Both are measurable on the holdout. Adding dots to a fixed belief surface only ever lowers DTI, because each one costs 0.2 whether or not it earns credit.
