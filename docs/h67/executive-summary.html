H66-C (2026-10-12) — the scarp-channel candidate, and why there is no download

Another session implemented the native-resolution LiDAR scarp-channel lane end to end and built a unique, format-valid GeoTIFF (gems66-h66c-scarpgap-42000-20261012T021141Z-4023a1c1ef59.tif, sha256 9bfe2d404a81f02598576000ce41ce8082475768fde5a29f346d2dadcec3d34a). It passes the shared validator with 15 gating checks and zero failures, and its leakiest single feature reaches AUC 0.646 against a 0.90 threshold. It is not offered here.

The reason is the gate, not the file. On instrument N (SGMC off-catalogue faults inside LiDAR-covered held-out blocks) it measures 0.0979, 95% CI [0.0623, 0.1219], against frozen baselines of 0.1195 (anderson), 0.1104 (h33-2-b2) and 0.1065 (d28). On instrument S it measures 0.1231 against 0.1688 (d28), but that instrument is anti-correlated (ρ = −0.708) with the owner-reported live scores, so it cannot be used to argue a win. Neither instrument is a validated surrogate; see IR-66-INSTR-01. Because the brief says to offer a download only when the gate is met, the bytes stay in artifacts/h66c/ for audit, with their hashes published on the overview.

What would have to change for it to be worth a slot: an organiser receipt for a candidate with a different field confirming the −0.126-per-unit-f200 dose response is causal; a local instrument whose rank correlation with live scores survives partialling out dot count; and two independent scientific-lead reviews. None of the three exists today, so the honest default is to keep the slot.

1. What the portal accepts

From the official submission-format rules: a single-band float32 GeoTIFF, EPSG:32611 (UTM zone 11N), 100 m resolution, the same bounds as the training data, values in [0, 1], and null/NaN outside the study area. A .zip containing one GeoTIFF is also accepted. The portal's own rejection message Predicted values must be in range [0, 1] means a value fell outside [0, 1] — the file published here is checked for exactly that at build time (see the gate table above).

3. Budget and rules that matter

  • Up to three feedback submissions per week.
  • You must choose one submission before the deadline; it is scored in both prize rounds, and you choose without seeing your private-test result.
  • The Initial Prize Round is scored against a fixed private set of expert-labelled new faults. The Final Prize Round re-scores the same file against an expanded label set built by an expert panel that reviews every team's predictions — so genuinely new, defensible fault candidates can pay off twice.
  • The rules require a narrative disclosure of generative-AI use. This repository's pipeline was written by an AI agent working from the public competition pages and public USGS/GDR data; say so truthfully and specifically in your narrative.

4. What is not cleared

Historical artifacts under artifacts/lidar_lane/, artifacts/h66-2/ and evidence/archives/h66-1/ are audit-only. They are not published here and must not be uploaded.
